Final team decision: Evaluate SaaS and hybrid options

SaaS vs. Hybrid Comparison

  • SaaS: Lower upfront cost and faster rollout, but less customization and ongoing subscription fees.
  • Hybrid: Keeps sensitive patient data on-site and allows more customization, but costs more to set up and maintain.
  • Key risk: Compliance with healthcare data privacy requirements applies to both options.
